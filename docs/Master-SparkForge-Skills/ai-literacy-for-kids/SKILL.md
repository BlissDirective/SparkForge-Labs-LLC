## Weighted practice points

- **(weight 13)** Critical thinking about AI outputs

- **(weight 12)** Safety: privacy, data, hallucinations
- **(weight 11)** Classroom integration & teacher readiness

- **(weight 11)** Hands-on making (train tiny models / prompts)

- Age-gate advanced tools; parent visibility where needed.
